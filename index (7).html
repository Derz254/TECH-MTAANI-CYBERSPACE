Privacy & document handling

Tech Mtaani Cyberspace is an independent digital service assistance provider in Likoni, Mombasa, and is not a government website. We handle personal information for service requests in accordance with applicable Kenyan data-protection requirements.

Information we collect

We collect the contact details, service category, request description and any optional supporting document you choose to provide. Please do not send passwords, PINs or one-time passcodes.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to your request, communicate about service progress, and maintain appropriate business records. We do not publish your request or supporting documents.

Retention and security

Access to submitted requests and documents is restricted to authorised personnel who need them to provide the requested service. Supporting documents are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the service and applicable recordkeeping obligations, then securely deleted.

Your rights and contact

You may request access to or correction or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law. For privacy questions or requests, email info@techmtaani.co.ke or call +254 792 257 998.
